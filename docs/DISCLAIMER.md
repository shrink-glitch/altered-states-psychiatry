## Disclaimer

**This is an educational resource for psychiatry trainees and clinicians.** It is not clinical guidance, and it does not replace clinical assessment, supervision, or local and national guidelines.

- **All cases are fictional composites.** They are written for teaching and do not describe real patients. Any resemblance to a real person is coincidental.
- **No substance-use guidance.** This resource does not provide, and will not accept contributions that provide, information on obtaining, preparing, dosing or using any psychoactive substance. Several substances discussed are controlled under India's Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, and under the laws of other countries.
- **Medication references are illustrative.** Where treatment is mentioned, it is at the level of approach (for example, "a mood stabiliser"). Prescribing should follow current guidelines and the clinician's own judgement.
- **No data collection.** The site does not collect, store or transmit personal data. Quiz answers stay in the learner's browser.
- **Not for patients in crisis.** If you are in distress, contact your local emergency services or a mental health helpline. In India, Tele-MANAS is available on 14416 or 1-800-891-4416.
